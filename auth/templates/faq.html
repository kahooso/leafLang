Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about leafLang learning system

How does the learning system work?

leafLang uses spaced repetition algorithm to help you memorize words effectively:

  • To Learn - New words you've added
  • Known - Words you're currently practicing
  • Learned - Words you've mastered

Words move between categories based on your answers. After 3 correct answers in a row, a word moves to "Learned".

How do I add new words?

You can add words in several ways:

  1. Manually through "Add Words" page
  2. Import from a text file
  3. Save words while reading (future feature)

All new words start in "To Learn" category and will appear in your practice sessions.

What's the recommended daily practice?

We recommend:

  • Beginners: 10 words/day (5-10 minutes)
  • Intermediate: 15-20 words/day (10-15 minutes)
  • Advanced: 20-30 words/day (15-20 minutes)

Consistency is key! Even 5 minutes daily is better than 1 hour once a week.

How does spaced repetition work?

Our algorithm shows you words at optimal intervals:

  • New words appear more frequently
  • Words you know well appear less often
  • Words you struggle with appear more often

This method helps transfer words from short-term to long-term memory.

How are words moved between categories?

The progression works like this:

  1. To Learn → Known: After first correct answer
  2. Known → Learned: After 3 correct answers in different sessions
  3. Learned → Known: If you forget a word

This system ensures you truly master each word.

Is my learning data private?

Absolutely! We value your privacy:

  • Your word lists are only visible to you
  • We don't sell or share your data
  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest

You can export your words anytime if you decide to leave.
